## 2024-05-24 - Accessible Hamburger Menus\n**Learning:** Lucide-react icons used inside buttons as the sole content (e.g., hamburger menus) need explicit `aria-label` on the button and `aria-hidden="true"` on the inner SVG. Otherwise, screen readers announce generic or unhelpful button descriptions. Adding `aria-expanded` further clarifies state.\n**Action:** Always wrap interactive utility icons (like Menu, X, Trash) in accessible button states with focus-visible rings and aria labels.
